Academic Eligibility, Weekend Schedule, Scholarships & How to Apply

Academic Eligibility Criteria

  • MBA (1.5 Years – For Business Graduates): 16 years of education or 4-year Bachelor's degree in a business-related field (such as BBA) with minimum 50% marks or 2.0 CGPA (additional deficiency courses may apply after reviewing the BBA transcript).
  • MBA (2 Years – For Non-Business Graduates): 16 years of education or 4-year Bachelor's degree in any non-business discipline with minimum 50% marks or 2.0 CGPA.
  • BBA Bridge Program / Weekend Options: 2-year Bachelor's degree in commerce or equivalent with at least 45% marks or 2.0 CGPA (where applicable).

Weekend Class Schedule

  • Saturday: 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM & 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM
  • Sunday: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM & 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Scholarships & How to Apply

  • Merit / SGPA Scholarships: SGPA 3.8+ (100% tuition fee) • SGPA 3.5–3.79 (50% tuition fee).
  • Step 1: Fill out the online admission application form on the SHU portal.
  • Step 2: Pay the Rs. 1,000 admission test fee via KUICKPAY.
  • Step 3: Appear for the admission test / interview and complete document verification.
