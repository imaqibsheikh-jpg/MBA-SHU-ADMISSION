        General Information

        🎓 Academic Eligibility Criteria

        • MBA (1.5 Years - For Business Graduates): 16 years of education or a 4-year Bachelor's degree in a business-related field (such as BBA) with a minimum of 50% marks or a 2.0 CGPA. (Additional deficiency courses may apply if prior BBA coursework had fewer or non-matching courses, to be determined after reviewing the BBA transcript.)
        • MBA (2 Years - For Non-Business Graduates): 16 years of education or a 4-year Bachelor's degree in any non-business discipline with a minimum of 50% marks or a 2.0 CGPA.
        • BBA Bridge Program / Weekend Options: 2-year Bachelor's degree in commerce or equivalent with at least 45% marks or a 2.0 CGPA (where applicable).
